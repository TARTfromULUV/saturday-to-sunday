SECTION ARTWORK

One wide image per section (roughly 16:9). Drop them here and reload.
Any of the names on a line will be picked up for that section.

  Scores          scores.png  or  scoreboard.png  or  results.png
  Top 25          poll.png  or  top-25.png  or  top25.png  or  rankings.png  or  the-top-25.png  or  staff-top-25.png
  The Column      column.png  or  the-column.png  or  weekly-column.png
  Game Stories    games.png  or  game-stories.png  or  game-story.png  or  recaps.png
  Playoff         playoff.png  or  the-playoff.png  or  cfp.png  or  bracket.png
  The Draft       draft.png  or  the-draft.png  or  nfl-draft.png  or  draft-board.png
  Recruiting      recruiting.png  or  the-recruiting.png  or  recruits.png  or  prospects.png
  The Carousel    carousel.png  or  the-carousel.png  or  coaching.png  or  coaches.png
  Statistics      stats.png  or  statistics.png  or  the-statistics.png  or  leaders.png
  Upcoming Picks  picks.png  or  upcoming-picks.png  or  the-picks.png  or  preview.png  or  looking-ahead.png
  The Money       money.png  or  the-money.png  or  nil.png  or  budgets.png

.jpg, .jpeg, .webp and .svg work too.
A section with no image falls back to its plain desk plate, so you can
add them one at a time.
