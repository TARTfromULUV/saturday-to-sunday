WRITER PHOTOS

One image per writer, named after them. Settings prints the exact filename
next to each name as you type it.

With the writers this build ships with, that is:

  Ray Halloran      ray-halloran.png
  Dele Okafor       dele-okafor.png
  Marco Vetrano     marco-vetrano.png

Rename a writer in Settings and the expected filename changes with them.
Square images look best - they are drawn small and round-cornered.
A writer with no image gets a monogram instead.

NFL staff (the NFL site's masthead, Settings > NFL > NFL staff):

  Tom Varga      tom-varga.png
  Nick Castellano     nick-castellano.png
  Joe Pruitt     joe-pruitt.png
  Sam Okafor     sam-okafor.png
  Walt Kessler     walt-kessler.png

.png, .jpg or .webp all work. Drop them here (or in your data folder's
branding/staff) and they appear next to the bylines and in The Regulars.
