# Update log

What changed in each version, newest first. The app shows this in
**Settings ▸ Update log**.

## 1.4.2 — 2026-10-20 — First run

- **No false update on a fresh ZIP.** A ZIP has no recorded build, and every branch commit counted as new, so a fresh install said "A new version of S2S is ready" on its first launch. An unknown build now goes by the version number; git checkouts still compare commits.
- **Setup errors in plain words.** Add a save strips the quotes from "Copy as path", an empty box gets focus instead of an error, "No saves found" says where saves live and that console saves can't be read. If the ChatGPT sign-in window can't open, the card points to `install-chatgpt.bat`.
- **Double-click while S2S is running** opens the running copy instead of a port error. `--help` prints usage.
- **Empty News and Your Team** say what to do first.
- **The docs match the app:** Install update and the Releases list instead of settings that are gone; the missing `API-KEY.md` is back; Help says the public download never needs a GitHub sign-in.

## 1.4.1 — 2026-10-20 — The hero rule, the brochure, the site

- **The front page by the moment.** One cover, and a switch on it: **National** or **your team**. National is the week's lead across the sport; your team puts the beat writer's notebook on your game in the frame, with your scoreline. It remembers which you prefer, per league. The preseason reads like a season preview, Signing Day and the draft lead with their own module, Super Bowl week is the matchup; the rails that have nothing to say that week are cut.
- **The picture owns its frame.** A real photograph on the front page never carries text: the kicker and the switch sit above it, the headline and the dek under it. Without a photo a plate drawn from the teams takes the same slot (their colours, their logos, the score). The band of numbers under the hero is gone; a game piece keeps its scoreline.
- **Drawn thumbnails, no words.** The top five is ranks and logos, the matchup two logos and a bar, the rest a logo with a glyph for what happened: the swap arrows for a trade, the cross for an injury, the stars for a commit, the card for the draft, the bars for the money. The card prints the desk under the picture.
- **The Championship cover** matches the game's championship week, prints the line as text with the network, and carries The year.
- **The held class, three states.** The college board drifts a little a week, a draft file is exact, and the draft releases it.
- **Branding packs.** Writer portraits come as a pack you add from Settings; the logo pack is an optional add-on; the stock ten writer headshots ship again.
- **Start with Windows works.** It goes through the registry's Run key now. **Desktop shortcut** is explained where it is turned on: an S2S shortcut lands on the desktop, and S2S has to be open for the phone to read the paper.
- **The ticker keeps rolling** until the mouse moves onto it, not the moment it passes over.
- **About** has LinkedIn and Discord, and the brochure as a download.
- **Two PDFs in the docs folder:** the brochure (what it is, why, how it works, where it is going) and a one-page quick start. Both linked from the README.
- **A landing page** in the docs folder, for GitHub Pages: the paper doing the thing, how it works, the desks on both sides, get it.

## 1.4.0 — 2026-10-19 — The new look

- **A new look around the paper.** The app around the newspaper is rebuilt in one design, dark first (light still works). The front page, the articles and the game stories keep their print look. Colour now means something: the league (red for college, blue for the NFL), a team, or a kind of row, never decoration.
  - **The top of the app:** a dark bar with a stripe in the league's colour; the tab you're on is underlined in it, and a tab's views are chips.
  - **Every page opens on a plate** in the league's colour: the page in big condensed type, the week in its label, and up to three numbers from your save. Scores counts the finals, the thrillers (and how many went to OT) and the upsets. Standings shows your place, your record and the best record. Rankings shows No. 1, where you are and the biggest riser. Stats shows the passing, rushing and receiving leaders.
  - **Scores:** each final is a row striped in the winner's colour, with the loser in grey and your game first and highlighted. Upset, Thriller and Runaway are tags.
  - **Team pages:** the team's plate in its own colour, with its logo, record, streak and rank (a light colour like gold gets dark text). The scoring is in tiles, and the overview reads as rows: the staff, the leaders, injuries in red (out for the season) or orange (shorter), signings and extensions in green. The beat reporter's notebook keeps the paper's type.
  - **Settings:** a plate with your saves, this month's usage and whether the app is current. Get set up is a progress bar that keeps only the steps left. Each save carries its league's stripe.
  - **The Wire, Coaches and the Draft hub:** moves are striped by kind; each staff card is striped in its team's colour; filter chips match the tabs.

- **The NFL draft class stays put.** Playing the two games hand in hand, the college side moves into its next year while Madden is still in the playoffs, and the NFL board swapped to next year's names. Now the class is held for the Madden season: a draft file exported from your college dynasty is the class as soon as it exists, imported into Madden or not; the college board or MyDynasty's projection still updates it week to week, but stops once the college moves on to a new year. The hold lets go when the class is drafted (its players are rookies) or the next Madden season's games start.
- **Where the class comes from, on the Draft tab and in Settings.** One line says what the board reads (the draft file and its folder, MyDynasty's projection and its folder, or your college site's board), whether it's imported, and when it's being held, with a link to Settings, which shows the same line.
- **The front page always has a cover picture.** The lead story always opens with an image. On weeks with no game to put on the scoreboard (the preseason, quiet offseason stages), it's a photo of the program the lead is about when the photo pack is installed, and otherwise a cover made from that team's colour and logo. When the headline names two teams playing each other, the cover shows both. The team is the first one named in the headline, else in the line under it, else your team.
- **Caught up when the schedule changes.** When a schedule generator (MyDynasty's, for one) rewrites the season after S2S has already written the week, S2S checks that issue against the new schedule. If a preview, an opponent or a week no longer matches, it rewrites the issue by itself (Write new weeks automatically on) or puts a note on the front page with a **Rewrite it** button (off). A change the issue never mentioned leaves it alone and costs nothing.
- **The offseason runs on events, not weeks (college).** The four Offseason Recruiting weeks are one window: Generate takes each week in and publishes nothing, and the window's story runs at **National Signing Day**, now with the flips (who committed somewhere and signed elsewhere, biggest names first). The other stages still get their issue: Season in Review, Players Leaving, Signing Day, Training Results and Encourage Transfers. The last stage reads **Encourage Transfers** (it showed "Offseason · Stage 9").
- **The carousel at a glance.** Wire ▸ Coaches ▸ Carousel opens with the year in four squares (head coaches out, with fired, retired and to the NFL under it; head coaches hired; coordinator hires; extensions), then an **In | Out** toggle that shows one list at a time.
- **Which league is ahead, in the offseason.** The badge on the NCAA | NFL toggle shows the stage when the league ahead is in its offseason (+PL, +REC 1, +NSD) instead of a count of weeks. The calendar of where each league is now opens from an ⓘ beside the toggle, so tapping the badge flips leagues like the rest of the pill.
- **The offseason by name.** The banner, the issue labels and the archive name College Football's offseason stages the way the game does: Players Leaving, Offseason Recruiting 1 to 4, National Signing Day, Training Results, Encourage Transfers (short forms on a narrow phone: PL, Rec 1, NSD). It said "Offseason 22" before. Older issues pick up the names too. An NFL franchise's offseason stages are numbered until their names are confirmed. The CFP rounds read Quarterfinal and Semifinal in the banner.
- **A new bottom bar.** Your team's logo and name take the first slot and open Your Team. **Home** (was Front) moves to the menu up top, first in line. Scores stays in the bar.
- **A steady price for a point.** Business ▸ Spending, player cards and the Business desk price the game's program points at one price, set once in your first season (that season's biggest quarterback NIL deal is the Settings number, $12M by default) and grown 3% a year after, whatever the game's deals do. A program's budget, its NIL and its other spending are its points at that price, so they always add up; each player's NIL deal is his share of his team's NIL, weighted toward the stars, so a roster adds up to its team. The page says what a point is worth. Change Settings ▸ Dollar figures and every season is priced again from the first. Seasons recorded before this update keep the dollars they had.
- **The economy tool at the same rate.** Zip's Economy Overhaul's donor wire and Business tabs now price its points at the paper's rate for that season, so a point is never two different amounts on one page.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-12 — Madden 27, one universe

- **Your coaching tree, and where your assistants went:** Wire ▸ Coaches ▸ Carousel now shows **Your coaching tree**: every coordinator who served under your head coach and where he is now (his new job, or retired, in the NFL, out of coaching). Every coach card's tree says the same for its branches. With MyDynasty, its carousel record fills in the staffs and the moves.
- **The coaching pipeline (MyDynasty's grad assistants):** the Carousel tab lists each class of players who went into the grad-assistant program and the year they come back as coaching candidates, yours first. A coach who came up that way shows where he played on his card, and the carousel piece says so when one is hired, with your own program's staff moves in a paragraph of their own.
- **MyDynasty's coaching carousel, written up the moment it runs:** when MyDynasty runs the carousel, the next issue leads with an in-depth carousel piece built from MyDynasty's own moves. It says why each coach is out (fired, contract not renewed, retired, left for the NFL), his last record and season grade, each new deal's length, who was poached or promoted, the coordinator moves, the extensions and the players following their coach. With **Write new weeks automatically** on, the issue writes itself when the run lands. Otherwise Generate carries it. **Wire ▸ Coaches ▸ Carousel** shows the full run at once (Out, In, Following their coach), and the moves join each coach's card.
- **Season in review names the conference champions:** the almanac looked for the title games by a "Championship" bowl name, which the save never gives them, so it reported none. It now reads them from conference championship week.
- **One frame on every page:** every page now wears the branded banner (your network · the league, then the page's name), not only the desk fronts. Awards, Coaches, News, Recruiting, Transfers, History, Business, Settings and the rest no longer have a plain ruled title. The order is the same everywhere: the page's tabs, the banner, the week, then the page's own buttons (Big board / By position, Of the week / Heisman, the Moves lists, a team's Season / Dynasty) as one row of buttons, then This week's piece and the blocks. Team pages wear the banner in the team's colours with its logo. Your own section artwork still replaces the drawn banner.
- **Playoff seeds, everywhere:** the bracket is seeded the way the playoff is drawn. The four byes are 1–4, each first-round host is 5–8 and its visitor 17 minus that (5 v 12, 6 v 11, 7 v 10, 8 v 9), and the quarterfinals pair 1 with 8/9, 2 with 7/10, 3 with 6/11 and 4 with 5/12. Once the field is set, a playoff game's teams show their seed instead of their poll rank on Scores, Picks and the score ticker, so a 12 seed ranked 14th in the poll reads 12 there just as on the bracket. The writers are told each side's seed and call playoff teams by it.
- **Tidier desk headers:** sub-tabs are a slim underlined row, the week's piece is a one-line **This week** strip, and section notes wrap under the title instead of being cut off on a phone. The desk banners (Draft Central, Expert Picks, Scoreboard…) stay.
- **Big Board, one line a prospect:** rank, logo, name, position · class · school, grade and the mover arrow. Tap a row for his stat line, scouting tags and a link to his scout card.
- **No more early players of the week (NFL and college):** the game names its players of the week as soon as it sims the AI games, before you've advanced past the week. Awards now never shows the week your save is on, and any that slipped in earlier are removed on the next Generate.
- **Awards ▸ Of the week labels the postseason by name:** Conference Championships, Selection Week, First Round, Quarterfinals, Semifinals and National Championship for college; Wild Card, Divisional, Conference Championships and Super Bowl for the NFL. No more Week 17 or Week 18.
- **Awards show the winners:** once the game gives out its season awards, the Heisman watch tab becomes **Heisman** (Award race becomes **Awards** in the NFL). The winner sits at the top with a star, and the race below is the final standings. Awards already given are starred in the award chips.
- **Banner playoff weeks fixed:** the banner skipped Selection Week, so every playoff week read one round ahead (Bowl Week 1 said Quarters). The banner now names a playoff week from its games: Bowl Week 1 is the First Round, Bowl Week 2 the Quarterfinals. The week between the conference title games and the bowls reads Selection.
- **Selection Week scoreboard:** a week with no games now shows the last games played (the conference championships) with a note, not an empty scoreboard.
- **The CFP bracket is back for the playoff itself.** From Selection Week on, the seeded bracket stays on the Playoff page: seeds, byes, the first-round matchups, and each quarterfinal's bowl. It fills in with scores as the rounds are played, with the loser greyed out, through the semifinals to the national champion. Seeds are kept from week to week, so they don't shift as the poll moves.
- **Selection Week front page:** the full bracket ("The field is set") sits right under the lead story, with the first four out underneath. Once the games start, the front-page box shows who's still alive.
- **College postseason: the playoff first, the bowls after.** Expert Picks in a playoff week features every game of the round (CFP First Round, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, National Championship) and nothing else. The bowls get a short **Around the bowls** section of one-line picks, however highly their teams are ranked. On Scores and Picks, the playoff games sit in their own **CFP <round>** box above the bowls, and each card shows its bowl's name. Game stories in a bowl week cover the playoff, the New Year's Six and your own bowl, and the rest stay on the scoreboard.
- **Banner shows the game's week:** the masthead now reads the week your console is on (Week 18 when the game is in Week 18, Conf on conference championship week), while the stories still cover the week before, so nothing you haven't played leaks. Hover the week to see both. Shows up on your next Generate.
- **Banner fits on small phones:** short stage names (Conf. Champ, or just Conf and Wk 18 on the narrowest screens), so the ESPN and S2S logos are never cut off.
- **Leagues lined up by the calendar:** the "+N wks" badge on the NCAA | NFL switch compares where each league is on the real calendar, not the week numbers (college conference championships are early December, NFL Week 15 mid-December; the college playoff and the NFL's late weeks no longer read wrong). Tap the badge for **Where each league is**: both seasons on one calendar, each league's stage and rough date, and how many weeks to advance to line them up.
- **Settings ▸ Features:** a new tab with everything S2S does, grouped (the paper, scores and stats, the draft, college, NFL, both leagues, your setup), each with a **Go** button to where it lives.
- **Update log tab removed:** what changed stays in CHANGELOG.md in the app folder. Settings is now Settings · Features · Help · About.
- **Old settings cleared out:** Watch the save (always on), Update the app automatically (Install update replaces it), the Hall of Fame bars (the defaults stand) and the Recruit-to-Pro labels switch (always shown) are gone from the code, not just hidden.
- **About ▸ Roadmap:** the next phase is an owner and head coach mode, tagged **Next phase**.
- **NFL cap, locked:** each season's league cap (the game's own, the same for all 32) is worked out once and kept, then every club's room is figured from it each week. A misread can no longer print a room past the cap (the Dolphins' "1693" is gone), and the Yearbook's cap-room list leaves out any old bad figure. The cap sheet and Cap space say **Locked**.
- **NFL Contracts, regrouped:** **The big deals** on top (this year's five, watched all season; a chip switches to **Biggest on the books**), **New deals** right under it as the wire (the week each first showed up, newest first, five then Show all), then **Value** with a **Bargains | Burdens** toggle.
- **NFL Values (was The books):** every club's value split the way the business press does it: **Sport** (the league's shared money), **Market** (its real TV market rank), **Stadium** and **Brand**, with a one-line reason ("No. 2 TV market · new SoFi Stadium"). Still an S2S estimate, built from the club's real stadium, market, fans and record.
- **A club's Business tab:** **The front office** first, then **What the club is worth** (the four parts, each with its reason), then the cap sheet and the books.
- **Settings, reorganised:** five sections, in the order you use them.
  - **Universe** sits a gap under the tabs and is always open to a glance: each league with its week, a **↺ Rewind** link right under the week, and **+ Add a save**. Open a league for **Every week** (one row, a dial for NCAA and one for NFL, adding up the extras) and **Ratings** (NCAA and NFL Show/Hide in one row), then College and NFL: team, your team pieces, the extras, save, MyDynasty, the economy tool (Dollar figures), staff, and Remove at the bottom.
  - **Writer** has everything that uses your plan: the account, auto-writing, and Features: SportsCenter (length and voices, one setting for every league), photos, drawn thumbnails, comments, sub-tab pieces, and Storylines.
  - **Usage** is just the meter, the renew day and week by week. **On your phone** explains Tailscale in plain words (what it is, what you get, what you need). **This device** has Opens on, appearance, the version, shortcuts and Clear cache.
  - Gone: Watch the save (always on now), the outside recruiting and carousel switches (they follow what MyDynasty runs), the Hall of Fame and Recruit-to-Pro options (defaults), and Update the app automatically (the red dot on the gear and Install update replace it; the app no longer installs on its own). TV rights and Realignment arc show as **Coming soon**.
- **Help, rewritten:** follows Settings section by section (Universe, MyDynasty and the economy tool, Writer, Usage, phone and Tailscale, updates), plus reading the paper and troubleshooting.

- **MyDynasty runs the college draft:** with MyDynasty installed, its projected class *is* the Big Board: its order, its scouting grades and its class labels (redshirts tagged **RS**, so redshirt sophomores like the ones it has in its first round now show up). Juniors it projects to stay in school move to a new **Next year's class** list under the board (top 10, Show all). The mock drafts, Draft stock and the writers all work off the same order; the writers credit it as "the projections". Without MyDynasty, the site's own board is the fallback, as before. The "players are draft-eligible" line is gone. Once the draft runs, results still come from the Madden side.
- **Draft stock:** a **Risers | Fallers** toggle shows one list at a time; the This week / This season / All-time chips stay.
- **NIL, paid against valuation:** the player card puts **Paid this year** beside his **Market valuation** (both on the Business tab's money model), a bar for the share, and one line of read: below-market deal, team-friendly, at market, or a premium deal to keep him.
- **Mock drafts:** no "Draft class: your college board" note and no "Best player still on the board" line under pick 32.
- **Sub-tab bars:** the pressed tab stays inside the rounded bar on a phone, and the bar has room below it, so it no longer sits on the card underneath (Wire, Settings and the rest).
- **NIL in dollars, the Business tab's way:** a college player's NIL now uses the same money model as Business ▸ Spending (dynasty points priced on the season's curve), so his deal adds up exactly to his team's NIL total. The card reads **Six figures · ≈ $450K a year** with a ✓ when a deal pays him (or *no deal yet*), a bar up to the season's top quarterback deal, and **Worth ≈ $X on the open market** from his base NIL value. The tier is set by the dollars. No random swing, so the player and the team book always agree.
- **Player cards, tidied:** NFL Overview drops Bio (the banner already says who he is); how he got here and his badges ride with the Draft block. **Honors** now shows **This season** and **Career** (season awards on file, past players of the week counted, Pro Bowls). NFL **Scouting** reads Progression, Skills, Scouting note, Pro Day. College Bio shows the recruit's **stars** alone (a transfer has its own block), no draft tag, and **NIL as a tier** (four, five, six or seven figures, with a bar and a tick when a deal pays him) instead of two dollar figures.

Overview is the man: **Bio** (college: how he came in, national rank, personality, deal breaker, NIL; NFL: personality and how he got here), Transfers, his career and season, Honors, the Draft stock at a glance (college) or his contract, moves and draft (NFL), Health and the news. Captain, holdout and trade badges stay; size, class, hometown, recruit stars, the draft and Pro Bowls move to a new **Bio** block at the top of Overview. Hurt? - **Profile gets Transfers** (every school he's played for, by year) and clearer NIL: **NIL deal · paid now** (what a school's deal pays him) and **NIL market value** (what the game says he's worth); a player can have either or both. **Health** shows on every current player, "Healthy" when there's nothing on file.

- **College injuries fixed:** Wire ▸ Injuries now reads the whole league's injury list (the same one the player card uses), not just the teams on that week's team pages.
