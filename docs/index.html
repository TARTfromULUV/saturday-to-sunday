1 The paper

Your league.
A paper every week.

Play a week. S2S reads your save and writes the issue: the column, the scores, the polls, the recruit who flipped, the rookie who went in the first round.

Written about your league, because it is your league. National, or flipped to your team with one switch. Real game photos for every program ship with the app.

2 In your pocket

Your dynasty.
In your pocket.

12:13 on a Tuesday. Set the recruiting board, answer the AD’s calls, read what your league is saying. Tonight, it’s all in your save.

It runs on your gaming PC and lives on your phone: scan one code, add it to your Home Screen. Setup is three steps, and S2S finds the rest: your dynasty, your franchise, MyDynasty, Zip’s economy.

At homeSame Wi-Fi as the PC. Nothing to install. Two minutes.

3 One universe

Saturday’s recruit.
Sunday’s rookie.

Add your CFB 27 dynasty and your Madden 27 franchise. They link up, and your players follow you to the NFL.

Each league keeps its own record book and history. The draft is where they meet: the class you recruited, drafted; the Hall of Famer you inducted on both sides.

4 AD mode Beta

Run the
business side.

You’re the athletic director. The coaching (game plans, recruiting, the depth chart) still happens in the game. AD mode reads it and doesn’t run it. You spend what you raised last year, and you raise next year’s.

A week is four stops on your phone: the Marketplace, Game Day, Coach, then Send. Ten sponsorships to sell, from the jersey patch to pouring rights. Reach out, call, sit down when they’re ready, and shake before their patience runs out. Price the ticket, pick a promotion, fill your box, and answer the paper’s writers.

Theo advises you, and is sometimes wrong. Your market analyst and your game day director only sharpen a number, and a better hire costs more. Discover shows what fans, partners and other schools are saying. The board sets a goal, the Recap grades your year, and the Winter Meetings set up the next one.

5 Into your save Beta

Send it
into the game.

Send closes your week, and it says exactly what goes into your dynasty file before you tap it. Most weeks, nothing does.

Once a year, the part of last year’s raise you put into the game is written into your school’s budget as program points. And if you turn them on, the league’s grades move with every Send: yours with your business, every other school’s with its results.

A backup first, every change read back. S2S never writes while the game is running. If a change doesn’t stick, the backup goes back and the week stays unsent.

Why I built this

The app I needed

I’m 38 with three kids. I don’t have time to sim a season and then scroll through menus to find out what happened.

What I wanted the next morning was a paper: the front page, the scores, the column, the recruit who flipped, the rookie who just went in the first round. Written about my league, because it is my league.

When I saw what Zip built, I knew it was the foundation. He had done the hard part. I had to go above and beyond with it: add the Madden side, link the two games, and make the whole thing read like it was real.

Get it · free for your own league

Download. Open START HERE. Done.

  1. DownloadThe green Code button ▸ Download ZIP, then right-click ▸ Extract All somewhere you’ll keep it, like C:\S2S. The folder holds START HERE, S2S, and one folder of S2S files you never need to open.
  2. Open START HEREIt looks like a phone. Its first step: double-click S2S. If Windows says “protected your PC”, More info ▸ Run anyway. It installs what it needs; when Windows asks about Node.js, tick both and Allow.
  3. Five steps, done for realS2S opens its own setup phone: it finds your save, connects ChatGPT (free), shows your phone’s code and turns on AD mode if you like, then sets itself to start with Windows. Each step ticks as it’s done.
