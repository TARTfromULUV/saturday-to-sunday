# S2S 1.6.0

The beta: a few friends get the rebuilt Front Office (AD mode) and Coach mode, and everyone gets a paper that's had a month of work.

## New, in the beta download

* **The Front Office opens.** Office sits in the bottom bar (tagged BETA) once S2S has read a college week. The beta stays on through self-updates. To leave it, delete `beta.txt` from your data folder.
* **AD mode, rebuilt.** A few calls before each kickoff, about money, people, the board and recruiting. Each comes with your people's advice and what each answer costs. A start mid-season opens with Catch me up.
* **Coach mode.** On a college league, the head coach's week sits beside the AD's: recruiting hours, pitches, visits, offers, the talent tree (its switch is on the office's Send page; it starts the next week). The Front Office is college-only for now.
* **The Front Office can write to your save, but only what you Send:** AD mode's pay, budget and staff moves, and Coach mode's recruiting moves. S2S refuses while the game is running. It backs the save up first, keeping the last five in an `S2S` folder beside it, then reads each change back and restores the backup if one doesn't stick. Everything else goes on a short to-do list for the game, and the next read checks it.

## New for everyone

* **Report a problem.** Settings ▸ Support ▸ Copy diagnostics puts what's needed to fix a bug in one block: versions, your leagues' weeks, the last run's log and the server's last errors. It never includes your save, a key or a sign-in.
* **The paper.** Home is a launch pad, the Universe tab is the same on both leagues, there are new draft stories on both sides, and the coaches, the carousel, recruiting and pipelines all got work. The full list is in the Update log.

## Updating

Settings ▸ Install update, or download the zip, close the app, unzip it over your folder and restart. Your leagues aren't touched.
