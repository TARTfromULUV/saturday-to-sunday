# S2S 1.5.0

The Front Office opens to everyone: run your school's athletic department from your phone, beside the paper that writes it up.

## New

* **The Front Office (AD mode).** Turn it on in Settings ▸ your league ▸ AD mode. It starts at your next preseason with **the call**: the year's arc, a goal and a lean for each bucket (Facilities, Game day, Marketing, People, Fundraising) and who runs each. The board signs off, and every week brings a short list of calls.
* **The Worklog** is the whole week in one place: each call opens in place with what it is, your people's advice, your options and what each one costs. Submit the week when you're done; reopen it until you advance in the game. Out of office hands the week to your people.
* **Your people, with faces and words.** Every call has an owner; the Worklog groups the week by who it waits on, and a call opens as a thread with them. Messages is where they, the board, your backers and the paper's writer talk to you; last week's conversations file themselves under Archived.
* **The apps are context.** Stadium, Game day, Marketing, People and Boosters each show their bucket: three numbers, the trouble risk, one list, one line of why it matters. The Marketplace is open to every brand (the sandbox is on, so any brand can be your jersey sponsor). Orders count as built the moment you send them. Radio reads the show the writers already made.
* **The Year and the Recap.** The Year is the big picture: the arc, the buckets, the grades, the money, the scorecard. The Recap grades the year against what the board expected, and next year's call starts from it.
* **The College Football Economy.** TV deals as real contracts, conference arcs, rumblings and moves, and a Business desk with its own menu.
* **Saved** keeps any article you bookmark, the same list on your phone and your PC.
* **Set up from your phone.** Double-click S2S and the PC shows one code. Scan it on the same Wi-Fi and your phone walks you through ChatGPT (free) and your saves, then into the office with a short tour. Turn AD mode on mid-season and the office opens at your next preseason; until then the paper talks about what's coming.

## Coming

The **Coming** folder on the grid holds mock-ups of what isn't built yet, each labeled up top and touching nothing: Head Coach mode (the war room and a recruit's file already read your save), the stadium builder, the mod shop (how an order reaches the game), firings, Social, and Write to my save. Settings ▸ What's coming lists them.

## Removed for now

* **Write to my save.** S2S never writes your game save. Books ▸ Match it in the game lists the few lines to set in the game by hand, and the next read ticks each one off.
* **Firings.** The president's confidence still shows; the school never lets you go.

## Changed

* **Rewrite only writes what changed.** A week the save hasn't moved past says **Up to date** and offers **Tidy up · only what changed** or **Rewrite every piece**.

## Updating

Download the zip, close the app, unzip over your folder, and restart. Your leagues aren't touched, and AD mode stays off until you turn it on.
