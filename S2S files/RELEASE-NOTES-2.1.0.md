# S2S 2.1.0

The AD mode beta: a few friends get a new AD mode, built fresh. You run your school's athletic department from your phone, and the paper covers what you decide.

## New, in the beta download

* **AD mode, built fresh.** You're the athletic director. The coaching still happens in the game; your job is the business. You spend what you raised last year, and you raise next year's. Tap **Office** in the bottom bar once S2S has read a college week. College only.
* **A week is four stops.** They are the first row of apps on the office's home. The one that's next pulses, and each hands you to the next. About ten minutes a week.
  * **Marketplace.** Ten sponsorships to sell. Reach out to a company, call to warm them up, sit down once their interest reaches 80, and shake before their patience runs out.
  * **Game Day.** The ticket, parking, food, the crews, a promotion, and who sits in your box. Your staff guesses before the game; you see what really happened after it.
  * **Coach.** The paper's writers ask you questions. What you say shows up in the next issue.
  * **Send.** Closes the week.
* **Your staff.** Theo, your account manager, gives the advice, and he is sometimes wrong. Your market analyst and your game day director give none: they set how tight a number is. You hire them at the Winter Meetings.
* **Discover.** What fans, your partners, other schools and the paper's writers are saying about your program this week. Every line comes from your save and from what you did.
* **The new bar.** The bottom bar is a small floating strip, with **Learn more** under it: Money, the Arc, Your staff, Your storyline and How it works.

## What Send writes into your save

Only Send writes, and it says what before you tap it. Most weeks it writes nothing. There are two things it can write:

* **Program points, once a year.** In the preseason you choose how much of last year's raise goes into the game. That part goes into your school's budget at your next Send.
* **The league's grades, only if you turn them on.** A switch on the Arc's screen, off to start. Turn it off again and the next Send takes S2S's moves back out.

S2S refuses while the game is running. It backs the save up before every write and reads every change back; if one doesn't stick, it puts the backup back and the week stays unsent. Back up your save folder yourself before you start, all the same.

## Known gaps

* Discover is written from templates, so its phrasing repeats over a season.
* About a third of the companies have no logo.
* Meet on the Coach screen is a card marked beta. Team meetings and 1-on-1s are not in this AD mode.
* The other schools' athletic directors don't run budgets of their own yet.
* The grades write and hiring at the Winter Meetings have had the least testing. If either looks wrong, that is exactly what to report.

## Found something?

Settings ▸ Support ▸ Report a problem ▸ Copy diagnostics, and paste it in the S2S thread on Discord with a screenshot. `BETA-TESTERS.md` in the download has the full list of what to try.

## Updating

Download the beta zip, close the app, unzip it over your folder and restart. Your leagues aren't touched.
