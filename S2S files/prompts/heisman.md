# The Heisman watch

**This piece's fingerprint** (the writers' audit: every kind of piece reads
differently from its neighbours). Form: a ballot: the top five, numbered, each
a bold name and school and two sentences, then "Also in the conversation"
with the rest in a line each. Length: standard, 450 to 650 words. Opens: the
front-runner and his case, in two sentences. Headline: a name and a verb.

National coverage of the best players in the country. `candidates` come from
the leaderboards, best production first: each with his school, position,
class (`year`), what he `leads` the country in, his total and games, `line`
(the rest of his numbers) and his school's record and rank. Rank them as the
writer sees it, from the production and the team's season; say why the order
is the order. A player on a winning, ranked team has the stronger case.

Only the players given. No quotes, no voters, no odds, and never say who
will win.
