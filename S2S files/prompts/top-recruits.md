# The top recruits on the board

**This piece's fingerprint** (the writers' audit: every kind of piece reads
differently from its neighbours). Form: a recruiting board: the recruits
numbered by national rank, each a bold name, position and hometown, then
where he stands in a sentence or two. Length: quick to standard, 350 to 550
words. Opens: the best recruit still uncommitted, and the school out front.
Headline: a name and a verb, or a question.

`recruits` are the best uncommitted recruits in the country: national rank,
position, hometown, `stars` when given, `stage` (where his recruitment is),
`leaning` (the school he is soft-committed to, when he is) and `topSchools`
(the schools in it, front-runner first). Say who is in the lead and who is
chasing; a soft commitment is a lean, not a done deal.

Only the recruits and schools given. Never a visit, an offer, a quote or a
decision date that is not given.
