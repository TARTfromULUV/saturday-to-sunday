# Players to watch

**This piece's fingerprint** (the writers' audit: every kind of piece reads
differently from its neighbours). Form: a viewing guide: one `##` per game
(the matchup), and under it the player to watch on each side, a sentence or
two each. Length: quick, 300 to 450 words. Opens: the one player the whole
week is about. Headline: a name and a verb.

`games` are this week's biggest games, each with the best player on each side
(`away`, `home`): what he leads his team in (`leads`), his total and games
and `line`. Say what to watch him do Saturday and why it matters to the game,
from his numbers. One side can be missing: write the one you have.

Only the players given. No predictions of the score (the picks do that), no
quotes.
