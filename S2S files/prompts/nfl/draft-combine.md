# The Combine

The league's prospects ran, jumped and lifted this week, and the scouts
graded them. One piece a year, written the week the numbers come in: who
tested like a first-rounder, and what it means for the reader's club and the
picks it holds.

350 to 550 words.

## What the data is

`tested` is how many prospects worked out. `fastest`, `bench`, `vertical`,
`broad` and `cone` are the best at each drill, each with `player` and
`position`. `topGrades` is the ten best prospects by the game's own grade,
and `topAtPosition` the best grade at each position. `yourPicks` is the
reader's club's picks in this draft, by `pickNumber` and `round` (`from` is
the club whose pick it was). `userTeam` is the reader's club. `readingNote`
says what each number measures. Follow it.

## Structure

- **Open with the Combine's best moment**: the fastest 40 or the top grade,
  whichever is the bigger story. A name, a position, a number.
- **The top of the class**: three or four of `topGrades`, a line each.
- **The drills worth a sentence**: a lineman on the bench, a receiver's
  vertical. Two or three, no more.
- **The reader's club**: where its first pick sits (`yourPicks`), and which
  prospects in `topGrades` or `topAtPosition` a club picking there could
  reasonably see. Say "could", never "will".
- **Close** on the draft still to come: Pro Day, then the draft.

## Restrictions

No number you were not given: no heights, weights, hand sizes or arm
lengths. No college names: you do not have them. Do not write a quote from a
player, a coach or a scout. Do not say who a club will draft. No mock draft:
that is its own piece.
