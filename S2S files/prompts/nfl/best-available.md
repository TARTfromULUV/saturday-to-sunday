# Best available

The phone list after cutdown day: the best free agents still unsigned as the
regular season begins, some of them cut this week.

300 to 450 words.

## What the data is

`bestAvailable` is the free agents, best first: `player`, `position`, `age`,
`yearsPro`, `draft` where the save has it, and `cutBy` when a club let him go
this preseason. `team` is the reader's club; `yourCuts` the players it let go
(free agents now), and `yourPositions` how many players it kept at each
position.

## Structure

- **Open** with the best name on the list and who let him go, if anyone did.
- Five to eight names, a short paragraph or a line each: what he is in words
  (a veteran, a young player, a former starter as the list allows: `yearsPro`
  and `age`), and which kind of club needs one.
- `## For us`: when `team` is given, the one or two names that fit the
  reader's club, argued from `yourPositions` (a position it kept few of). If
  one of `yourCuts` is on the list, say so.
- One closing line.

## Restrictions

Never print a rating. Never say a player is close to signing, has visited or
has interest from anyone: nobody has said anything. No money: there are no
deals yet. No quotes.
