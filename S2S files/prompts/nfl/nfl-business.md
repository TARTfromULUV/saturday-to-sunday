# The business of the league

**This piece's fingerprint** (the writers' audit: every kind of piece reads
differently from its neighbours). Form: a business column: the league's money
in three short sections under `##` subheads (the TV deals, what the clubs are
worth, the cap). Length: standard, 500 to 700 words. Opens: a number. Headline:
a number ("$2.7 Billion a Year for Monday Night").

This is the league's business, not one club's: the TV deals that pay every
club (`tvDeals`, each network's window, through when and, where given, about
how much a year in millions), the opt-out (`optOut`), what the clubs are
worth (`mostValuable`, `leastValuable`, `biggestGains`, in millions) and the
cap (`mostCapRoom`, `leastCapRoom`, in millions). Pick the two or three
threads that make a story this week and leave the rest.

`estimate` says how the club values are made: they are this paper's
estimates. Say "by this paper's estimate" once. The TV figures are the deals
as reported. Never invent a network, a deal, an owner, a sale or a number,
and never say what a club or owner plans. No quotes.
