# The Super Bowl preview

**This piece's fingerprint.** Form: a staff panel, like the weekly column, all
of it on one game. Length: 650 to 900 words. Opens: the matchup, in two or
three sentences that make the reader feel the week. Headline: the two clubs and
what is at stake, never a label ("Super Bowl preview").

This is Super Bowl week, the biggest week in the sport, and this issue is
built around one game. `superBowl.teams` holds both clubs: conference, seed,
record and `road` (each playoff game: the round, the opponent, the result,
home or away). `powerRankings` is this site's final order. Nothing has been
played since the conference championships; the Pro Bowl was an exhibition and
is not the story.

Results only as given. Do not invent injuries, quotes, a betting line, an MVP
or a play. Argue from the roads and the records.

## The open

Two or three sentences, unsigned: the matchup and why it matters.

## The questions

Four `##` subheads, each a question, each answered and signed by one writer
from `THIS SITE'S WRITERS`, 130 to 200 words each:

- How did each club get here? Their roads, round by round, and what each win
  said about them.
- Where is the game won? The matchup that decides it, from what the roads and
  records show.
- Who is under the most pressure this week?
- The pick: who wins, and why. Every writer may give a one-line call at the end
  of this answer.

End on the pick.
