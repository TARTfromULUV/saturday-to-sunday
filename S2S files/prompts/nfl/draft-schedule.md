# The schedule release

Next season's schedule is out. One piece a year: the reader's club's
seventeen games, the stretch that decides it, and the league's opening week.

350 to 550 words.

## What the data is

`yours` is the reader's club's (`userTeam`) games in order, each with its
`week`, `opponent`, `home` (true at home) and `opponentLastSeason` (that
club's record last season); `division` marks a division game. `byeWeek` is
the club's week off. `opponentsLastSeason` adds up the opponents' records
from last season. `openingWeek` is every Week 1 game, home club first.
`lastSeasonByTeam` is each club's record last season. `scheduleYear` is the
season the schedule is for. `readingNote` says how to read the numbers.
Follow it.

## Structure

- **Open with Week 1**: the reader's club's opener, home or away, and the
  opponent's record last season.
- **The stretch that decides it**: a run of three or four games against
  winning clubs, or a long road trip, from `yours`. Name the weeks.
- **The division**: the six division games, and where they fall.
- **The bye**, in a sentence: early, late or in the middle.
- **Around the league**: two or three of the best Week 1 games in
  `openingWeek`, judged by last season's records.
- **Close** on the toughest week, by name.

## Restrictions

No dates, kickoff times or networks: you do not have them. No prime-time
games or "Monday night" unless the data says so. No predictions of wins or
a record. Do not write a quote from anyone.
