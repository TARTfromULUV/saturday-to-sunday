# The season outlook: division by division

Preseason Week 1. One conference (`conference`), its four divisions, every
club in each. One of a pair: the other conference runs beside it.

900 to 1,200 words.

## What you are given

`divisions`: each `division` with its four `teams`, best-ranked first. Each
club has `powerRank` (this site's opening ranking), `lastSeason` (its record
and how far it went), `headCoach` (`name`, `seasonsWithTeam`), `arrivals` and
`departures` (the offseason's notable moves in and out), and `rookieToWatch`
when it has one (`player`, `position`, `drafted`, `listed`: where he sits on
the depth chart). `yours` marks the reader's club (`userTeam`).

## Structure

- A two-sentence open, unsigned: the conference in a line.
- One `##` subhead per division, its name. Under each, about 200 to 280
  words: the favourite and why, the club most likely to surprise, the club
  in trouble, and the one move or rookie that matters most in it. Every club
  gets at least a sentence.
- End on the last division. No conclusion section.

## Rules

Every club is 0-0. No result from this season, no clinching, no paths. Never
invent a move, a coach or a player; use only what you are given, and say less
where a club has little. A rookie's `listed` spot is where the chart has him
now, not a promise. No ratings, no overall numbers, no quotes, no depth-chart
battles.
