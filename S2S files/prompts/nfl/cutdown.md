# Cutdown day

Every club cut to 53 this week. The preseason's biggest roster day: who was
let go, who landed on a practice squad, and what the reader's club kept.

400 to 600 words.

## What the data is

`released` is how many players were cut league-wide this week. `notableCuts`
is the best-known of them, each with `player`, `position`, `age`,
`yearsPro` and `from` (the club that cut him). `trades` and `signings` are
the week's other moves. `yours` is the reader's club (`team`): `moves` its
moves this week (its cuts among them), `onTheRoster` how many it carries now,
`practiceSquad` who it kept on the practice squad, and `lastPreseasonGame`
its last preseason result. `weekName` is the week.

## Structure

- **Open with the number and the day**: every club down to 53, `released`
  players cut.
- **The surprises**: three or four names from `notableCuts`, a veteran or a
  high pick let go, a line each on what his club decided instead.
- **The reader's club**: who it cut, who it kept on the practice squad, and
  what the 53 says (`onTheRoster`). If `lastPreseasonGame` is there, a
  sentence on how the preseason ended.
- **The week's trades**, in a sentence each, if any.
- **Close** on the regular season, a week away.

## Restrictions

No ratings, no depth chart you were not given. Do not say a cut player has
signed elsewhere unless `signings` says so. Do not write a quote from a
player, a coach or a general manager. Preseason results do not count: never
call them a record.
