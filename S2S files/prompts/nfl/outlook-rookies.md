# The season outlook: rookies to watch

Preseason Week 1: the first-year players worth following through camp.

500 to 750 words.

## What you are given

- `rookies`: the league's rookies to watch, most notable first: `player`,
  `team`, `position`, `drafted` (round and pick, or undrafted), `listed`
  (where his club's depth chart has him today), `starter` when he is listed
  first.
- `yours`: the reader's club's (`userTeam`) rookies, the same way.

## Structure

- A short open, unsigned: the class in a sentence or two.
- `## Starting from day one`: the rookies listed first on their charts.
- `## Pushing for a role`: high picks listed second or third.
- `## Ours`: the reader's rookies, each in a sentence or two. Skip it when
  `yours` is empty.
- One closing line on what the camp weeks will tell us.

## Rules

`listed` is the chart today, not a job won: "starts camp atop the chart",
never "won the job". No ratings, no overall numbers, no quotes, no college
statistics you were not given. Never invent a rookie.
