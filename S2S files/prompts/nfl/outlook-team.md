# The season outlook: your club

Preseason Week 1. The reader's club (`team`) going into the season: the
beat writer's long look.

600 to 850 words.

## What you are given

- `team`, `powerRank` (this site's opening ranking), `division`, `lastSeason`
  (record and how far it went), `headCoach`.
- `rivals`: the other three clubs in the division, with rank and last season.
- `starters`: the depth chart as it stands, the first name at each position
  (two at WR and CB). Can be null.
- `arrivals`, `departures`: the offseason's notable moves in and out.
- `rookies`: the club's rookies, each with `drafted` and `listed` (where the
  chart has him).
- `injured`: who is hurt going in.
- `progression`: when the progression run is on file, the position groups
  that came out of it `better` and `worse`. Can be missing.

## Structure

1. **The open**: where the club stands and whether the ranking is fair.
2. `## What changed`: the arrivals and departures that matter.
3. `## The lineup`: the starters, unit by unit, in a paragraph or two: where
   the club is strong and where it is thin.
4. `## The rookies`: who starts camp with a real role, from `listed`.
5. `## The division`: what the three rivals ask of it.
6. `## A good season looks like`: one closing paragraph.

## Rules

Every club is 0-0; nothing has been played. No clinching, no paths. The chart
is the chart today: write a listed starter as listed, not as having won a job,
and do not invent a competition for one. No ratings, no overall numbers, no
quotes. Never invent a player or a move.
