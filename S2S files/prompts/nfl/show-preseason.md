# The preseason column

The flagship, in the weeks before the regular season starts. Same panel
format as the season column: the masthead's writers each take a question and
sign their answer. The subject is the season about to begin, argued from
`powerRankings` (this site's opening ranking of all 32, with each team's
division). Each preseason week has its own subject, named by `focus`.

500 to 750 words.

## Nothing has been played

- **Every team is 0-0.** No regular-season game has happened. Never write that
  anyone opened, won, lost, or is "off to" anything. Preseason games do not
  count and are not mentioned.
- **No clinching, no paths.** Nobody controls its own fate, has a path to a
  division title, or needs to win out. Those are December words.
- You have no result from last season. Do not reconstruct one.
- People come from what you are given. No invented quotes, sources, or
  depth-chart battles. No quotes at all: nobody has played. Moves come only
  from `moves`, the reader's rookies only from `yourDraftClass`.

## Not the same column twice

`recentHeadlines` are the last columns' headlines. Do not reuse their angle,
their lead team, the division they argued, or their wording. `alsoInThisIssue`
lists the other pieces in this issue: do not retell them.

## The open

Three or four sentences, unsigned: the shape of the league going in, from the
week's `focus`.

## The questions

Four `##` subheads, each a question, each answered and signed by one writer
from `THIS SITE'S WRITERS`, 120 to 180 words each. Use the set for `focus`:

- **favorites**: Who is the team to beat? Who is ranked too high? Who is the
  sleeper outside the top ten? Which conference is deeper?
- **new-faces**: Which club's offseason moves change it most? The best move of
  the offseason. The move that will not work. What changed for the reader's
  club. From `moves`; without it, argue from the rankings and say less.
- **divisions**: The best division, the worst, the closest race, and the one
  the rankings get wrong. Pick divisions the recent columns did not argue.
- **your-team**: The reader's club (`userTeam`): where it is ranked and
  whether that is right, what its division asks of it, the rookie or move that
  matters (`yourDraftClass`, `moves`), and what a good season looks like.

Never invent a player, a signing or a coaching change.

End on the last answer. No top ten this week; omit `analystTop10`.
