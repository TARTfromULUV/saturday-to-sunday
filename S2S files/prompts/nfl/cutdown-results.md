# Cutdown results: who made the team

The morning after cutdown day. Every club is at 53 and Week 1 is next. This
is the roster story of the summer told from the end: who came to camp and
stayed, who was let go, who landed on a practice squad, and the reader's
final 53.

500 to 750 words.

## What the data is

`comparedWith` says what the camp is measured against (each club's roster at
the start of camp, or, when that was not on file, the preseason's own
transactions). `league` counts it: `rookiesOnThe53`, `undraftedOnThe53`,
`cutToFreeAgency`, `toPracticeSquads`.

- `undraftedWhoMadeIt` and `lateRoundRookiesWhoMadeIt`: rookies who were in a
  club's camp and are on its 53 now, best first, with `team`.
- `notableCuts`: the best-known players cut this preseason who are free
  agents now, with `cutBy`.
- `landedElsewhere`: players who left one club's camp and are on another club
  now (`leftCamp`, `now`; `onPracticeSquad` when it is that club's practice
  squad). How they moved is not given: say they are with the new club, not
  that they were claimed or traded.
- `byClub`: every club's count on the 53 and its practice squad, and how many
  of its rookies made it.
- `yours` (the reader's club, `team`): `final53` by position (names in no
  particular order: it is not a depth chart), `rookiesWhoMadeIt`, `cuts`,
  `toPracticeSquad`, `practiceSquad`, `leftForOtherClubs` and
  `arrivedSinceCamp` (with `from`).

Any list can be empty. Write only the sections that have something.

## Structure

- **Open on the day**: every club down to 53, with the league's counts.
- `## Made it`: the undrafted and late-round rookies who survived camp, a line
  each for the best three or four, the rest in a sentence together.
- `## Let go`: three or four names from `notableCuts`, a veteran or a known
  name, each with the club that cut him. Then `landedElsewhere`, if any.
- `## Ours`: the reader's 53. Count it, walk it by position group in a short
  paragraph from `final53` (how many quarterbacks, receivers, linemen it
  kept), name the rookies who made it, the cuts and the practice squad.
- **Close** on Week 1.

## Restrictions

No ratings, no depth chart, no position battles: `final53` says who is on the
roster, not who starts. Never give a reason a player was cut or kept. Do not
say a cut player has signed anywhere. Preseason results do not count. No
quotes from anyone.
