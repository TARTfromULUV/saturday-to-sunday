# The season outlook: the league

Preseason Week 1's lead piece, in the column's slot: the whole league going
into the season. The rest of the outlook rides beside it in this issue
(`alsoInThisIssue`): the AFC and the NFC division by division, the reader's
club, the rookies to watch. Do not do their jobs: no division-by-division
tour, no long passage on the reader's club, no rookie list.

700 to 950 words.

## What you are given

- `powerRankings`: this site's opening ranking of all 32, each with its
  `division`.
- `lastSeason`: each club's record last season and how far it went (`went`).
- `offseasonMoves`: the offseason's biggest trades, signings and extensions
  (`kind`, `player`, `position`, `from`, `to`, `years`, `totalMillions`). Can be
  missing.
- `progression`: when the league's progression run is already on file, the
  clubs that came out of it most and least improved. Can be missing.
- `leagueNews`: the game's own news wire, when there is any.
- `userTeam`: the reader's club.

## Structure

1. **The open**, unsigned, three or four sentences: the shape of the league
   going in.
2. `## The team to beat`: the top of the rankings, and whether it is right.
3. `## The climbers`: two or three clubs set to be better than last season,
   from the moves and the rankings.
4. `## The fallers`: two or three set to slip, honestly.
5. `## The conference balance`: which conference is deeper, and why.
6. `## The question of the season`: one open question the season will
   answer. End on it.

## Nothing has been played

Every club is 0-0. Never write that anyone opened, won or lost anything this
season. No clinching, no paths, no "control their own fate". Preseason games
are not mentioned. No quotes. Never invent a player, a move or a coaching
change; people and moves come only from what you are given. No depth-chart
battles: the preseason log covers those from the charts in the weeks ahead.
No ratings or overall numbers.
