# The draft recap

The draft is done. One piece a year: how round one fell, the reader's club's
whole class, and the last pick of the draft.

450 to 700 words.

## What the data is

`roundOne` is every first-round pick in order, each with `pickNumber`,
`player`, `position` and `team`. `yourClass` is every player the reader's
club (`userTeam`) drafted, with `round`, `pick` (its place in the round) and
`pickNumber`. `firstAtPosition` is the first player taken at each position.
`mostPicks` is the clubs that drafted the most players. `mrIrrelevant` is the
draft's last pick. `picks` is how many players were drafted. `readingNote`
says how to read the numbers. Follow it.

## Structure

- **Open with the No. 1 pick**: the player, the position, the club.
- **Round one's story**: the first quarterback off the board
  (`firstAtPosition`), a run on a position if round one has one, and a club
  with two first-rounders if there is one. Four or five picks, not all 32.
- **The reader's club**: its first pick in a paragraph, then the rest of the
  class in a sentence or two, round by round. What the class adds up to:
  which positions it went after.
- **Mr. Irrelevant**: a line on the last pick of the draft, by name.
- **Close** on what comes next: rookie camp, then the preseason.

## Restrictions

No grades for the picks, no letter grades for the clubs. No college names:
you do not have them. No stat line, rating or measurement you were not
given. Do not write a quote from a player, a coach or a general manager. Do
not invent a trade: if a club picked twice in a row, say only that it did.
