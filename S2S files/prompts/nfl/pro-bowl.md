# The Pro Bowl

The week after the conference championships, the Pro Bowl gets its one
piece. A short sidebar, not the lead: the conference championships are this
week's story, and the Super Bowl is next week's.

200 to 350 words.

## What the data is

`rosters` holds the AFC and NFC Pro Bowlers, each with `player`,
`position` and `team`, and `firstTime` when it is his first. `byTeam` is the
clubs with the most players. `yourTeam` and `yours`, when present, are the
reader's club and its Pro Bowlers. `source` says whose rosters these are:
`game` means the game named them, and `projection` means this site's
projection. `sourceNote` tells you how to word that. Follow it exactly.

## Structure

- **Open with the reader's club** when `yours` has names: who made it, and
  what it says about their season. When it has none, open with the club in
  `byTeam` with the most players.
- **The first-timers** worth a sentence: a name, a position, a club.
- **Close in a line** on the game itself: AFC against NFC, a week before the
  Super Bowl.

## Restrictions

No stat line you were not given. No votes, vote counts or fan-vote totals.
Do not write a quote from a player or a coach. Do not treat the Pro Bowl as
the week's biggest news.
