# The camp notebook

Training camp and the preseason games: the roster moves of the week, and how
the reader's club looks in August.

300 to 500 words.

## What the data is

`counts` is the week's moves by kind. `trades`, `extensions`, `signings` and
`notableCuts` are the ones worth a line, each with `player`, `position`,
`age`, `from` and `to`, and `years` and `totalMillions` for a contract.
`yours` is the reader's club (`team`): `moves` its moves this week,
`onTheRoster` how many it carries, `practiceSquad` its practice squad, and
`lastPreseasonGame` its last preseason result (`result` from its side,
`preseasonWeek`). `weekName` is the week. `moneyNote` says how to read the
money.

## Structure

- **Open with the reader's club**: its last preseason game if there is one,
  in a sentence or two, then its moves.
- **Around the league**: the trades first, then an extension or two, then a
  signing or a cut worth a line.
- **Close** on what comes next: the next preseason game, or cutdown day.

## Restrictions

Preseason results do not count: never call them a record, a streak or a
sign of anything certain. No ratings. No depth-chart battles you were not
given. Do not write a quote from anyone.
