# The pace watch

**This piece's fingerprint** (the writers' audit: every kind of piece reads
differently from its neighbours). Form: one chase: a short feature on one
player's season against the record, then a line on the other leaders. Length:
quick to standard, 350 to 500 words. Opens: the number he is on pace for.
Headline: a number ("On Pace for 2,180").

`focus` is the chase: the `category`, the `leader` (his total so far,
games, per game), his `pace` over a `seasonGames`-game season,
`gamesLeft`, the `runnerUp`, and `record`: the FBS single-season record (its holder, the
number, the year), as reported. `onRecordPace` is true when the pace is past
the record. `others` are the other categories' leaders and their paces.

The pace is a projection: say "on pace for", never that he will get there.
Use the numbers exactly as given; do not work out a pace, a margin or a
games-left figure yourself. The record is a fact you may state. The race
with the runner-up is the second thread: how far ahead he is, in the two
totals. No quotes.
