# Update log

What changed in each version, newest first. The app shows this in
**Settings ▸ Update log**.

## 2.1.6 — The office in a week with nothing to do

- **The office's home in the postseason and the offseason is laid out as the office.** It said "Raising is locked" twice and ended in two plain buttons. Now it is the year so far in one card, four places to look as a row of apps (Marketplace, Money, Discover, Your staff), the mood, then what is new: the Winter Meetings, the Recap, the news and your storyline.
- **Money has a third tab: The numbers.** The five charts and the week's line of updates that 2.1.5 put in Discover live here now, every chart open, one under the other: the raise against the board's pace, game day, your partners, where the money comes from, and you and the field.
- **Discover is the stories and the people.** The Paper's newest stories run through the feed as posts: the writer who filed it, the story's own art, its headline, and the top reader comment the Paper prints under it. It is the same story, drawn as a post; a tap opens it in the Paper. The fans, your partners, other schools and the board post between them, and the mood stays at the top.

## 2.1.5 — Discover's numbers

- **The numbers, in Discover.** Five cards to swipe across, under the mood; tap one and it opens with its chart. Every figure is one AD mode already keeps.
  - **The raise:** what you have raised week by week, against the pace the board wants.
  - **Game day:** a bar a home game, the gate with its fan score.
  - **Your partners:** every partner's bar, the lowest first, with this week's move.
  - **Where it comes from:** the raise by source: sponsorships, the ad boards, game day, the postseason, the conference.
  - **You and the field:** your conference's TV money a school against every other conference's, and how many of your sponsorships are sold.
- **This week, in a line.** A row of updates above the cards: what you raised this week, how the fan score moved, a partner who moved, and what is still open.

## 2.1.4 — Discover is its own thing

- **Discover is one feed, and it is about you.** The Paper says what happened; Discover is how people feel about what you did. The News, Business and wire tabs are gone. It is posts: fans, your partners, other schools and your board.
- **The mood, at the top.** Fans, your partners and the board, each with a word and an arrow for this week, from numbers AD mode already keeps: the last home game's fan score, your partners' bars, and where you are against the board's goal.
- **More of your choices get a reaction.** Parking, food prices, the crews, a promotion, and what you told the press now draw posts, as the ticket price and a signing already did. A partner you have let slide says so.
- **A story is a share.** One of your stories is in the feed when somebody passes it on, and the Paper's Business stories from around the league are posted by the Paper; a tap opens the story there.
- **A player's card reads his stars** whether the save gives them as a word or a plain number.

## 2.1.0 — The AD mode beta

- **AD mode, built fresh (beta).** You run the business side of your program from your phone: you spend what you raised last year, and you raise next year's. The coaching stays in the game. It opens from **Office** in a beta download, once S2S has read a college week. College only.
- **A week is four stops.** Marketplace, Game Day, Coach and Send, the first row of apps on the office's home. The one that's next pulses, and each hands you to the next. About ten minutes a week.
- **Marketplace.** Ten sponsorships to sell, from the jersey patch and the field to pouring rights and the radio broadcast. Reach out to a company, call to warm them up, and sit down once their interest reaches 80. A star saves a company to a sponsorship. A sit-down is a negotiation: nothing tells you what a choice will do, so read what they say and shake before their patience runs out.
- **Game Day.** The ticket, parking, food, the crews, a promotion, and who sits in your box. You get your staff's guess before the game and what really happened after it.
- **Coach.** The paper's writers ask you questions each week. What you say moves something, and it shows up in the next issue.
- **Send, and what it writes.** Send closes the week and says what goes into your save before you tap it. Most weeks, nothing does. Once a year it writes the part of last year's raise you chose to put into the game, as program points. And, only if you turn them on (a switch on the Arc's screen, off to start), the league's grades. S2S refuses while the game is running, backs the save up first and reads every change back.
- **Hours, and the board's goal.** You have 100 hours a week in your first year and 60 after it. The board sets a goal each year from what you could sell; beat it and next year's budget is bigger.
- **Your staff.** Theo, your account manager, gives the advice and is sometimes wrong. A market analyst and a game day director give none: they set how tight a number is. You hire them at the Winter Meetings, and a better hire costs more.
- **Discover.** What fans, your partners, other schools and the paper's writers are saying about your program this week, each line from your save and from what you did. Its Business tab also carries the Paper's Business stories from around the league; a tap opens the story in the Paper.
- **The Marketplace stays open to look at.** In the preseason and once raising is locked, **See the Marketplace** on the office's home opens it. You can look around; the moves are greyed and say why.
- **The year.** Bowl weeks, the Recap that grades your year, and the Winter Meetings: the conference's votes, the TV deal, your hires, a job at another school.
- **The new bar.** The bottom bar is a small floating strip, with **Learn more** on a line under it (Money, the Arc, Your staff, Your storyline, How it works). The NFL side of a linked pair wears the same bar, with **Up to date** at the top.
- **The paper's top headline isn't a question every week.** The weekly column's headline is a question about one week in three and the plain news line the rest.
- **The score ticker stays whole on a phone.** It is slimmer and stays at the top with the masthead, so a short scroll no longer leaves a sliver of it under the phone's clock.
- **Built for the phone.** The office's screens are laid out for a phone first.
- **The guides are rewritten** for this AD mode: the README, the FAQ, START HERE, the website, the tour cards and BETA-TESTERS.md.
- **Known gaps in the beta:** Discover is written from templates and repeats over a season; about a third of the companies have no logo; Meet on the Coach screen is a card marked beta; the other schools' athletic directors don't run budgets of their own yet.

## 2.0.1 — The League, the AD loop, game day

- **The League.** A page for every school in your dynasty: five tabs, school against school, search any school or conference, and follow the ones you watch (they pin to Home with their week). Settings ▸ League view can hide the other schools.
- **AD mode, the loop.** Home is the week in one place: the Arc, the board's goals, the week, Money, and where next year's money is raised. Home also works as the menu, with links into Settings.
- **Money is two years.** This year is the game's Dynasty Blueprint, read only, with the share: one choice of how much of last year's raise goes into the game. Next year is what the season is raising, by source, against the board's fundraising goal. The other schools budget too, and a Settings choice sets how much a budget bucket's second grade counts. The Book, Budget and Business pages are folded into Money and the Marketplace; their old links still open the right page.
- **Boosters on hours.** Each week you have hours to spend (more with a better Brand and Conference grade): a call, a lunch, a game in your box. A booster's interest runs 0 to 100, and the week's ask is worth more the warmer he is. Boosters is one page.
- **The Marketplace and the deal board.** The Marketplace shows your sponsor money against the board's number, in three tabs: Sold, Open and In talks. Sponsors move on a deal board: stages, a shortlist that can change, four kinds of path, and a close that comes as a window.
- **You were just hired.** On a mid-season start, until your first week is sent or played, the last AD's deals can be dealt again as often as you like; three of them can be let go this season at no cost; and the boosters start where the last AD left them, one warm and one gone cold.
- **Around the league.** The College Wire's Business tab is back: every money story across the league, newest first, then TV deals and spending. The office's Arc shows the same stories. The Carousel shows eight rows, your school and conference first, with Show all.
- **A recruit's file.** A target on the recruiting board shows his size, state rank and two measurables from his player card. "His file" opens the card, and back returns you to his row.
- **Game day.** Six tabs with prices, crews and a fan score, and a business section on the box score: the gate priced by your Brand and what visits pay.
- **Setup help.** The download points to an online setup guide, for anyone who wants Claude to walk them through it.
- **Fixed:** a company shut out because its category is full at your Brand grade read "shut out to null". It now says why, with no year.
- **Fixed:** a home week could come with no Saturday plan from game day (about a third of first home weeks in testing). Every home week has one now.

## 2.0.0 — S2S 2.0

- **This is where the log starts.** Everything S2S does in 2.0 is listed in **Settings ▸ Features**, each with where to find it. From here on, every update is written up below.
- **The paper, both leagues.** Your college dynasty and your Madden franchise as one universe: the issue each week, SportsCenter the show, scores, standings, stats, recruiting, the draft and the history.
- **AD Mode and Coach Mode (beta).** Run the athletic department from your phone: the week as a news cycle (pressers, the night after the game, your account), sponsors as a season (negotiate, what they ask of you, how they feel, the Business Journal), boosters, staff, game day and the Arc. Coach mode adds team meetings and 1-on-1s. What you decide goes into your save only when you hit Send, with a backup first.
