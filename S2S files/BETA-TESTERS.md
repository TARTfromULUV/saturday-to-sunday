# S2S beta: AD mode

Thanks for testing. S2S writes a weekly sports paper about your CFB 27 dynasty and Madden 27 franchise, and you read it on your phone. This beta adds **AD mode**: you run your school's athletic department from the same phone, and the paper covers what you decide.

**AD mode: run the business side of your program.** You're the athletic director. The coaching (game plans, recruiting, the depth chart) still happens in the game. AD mode reads what you do there and doesn't run it. Your job is the business: you spend what you raised last year, and you raise next year's.

Your week is four stops, the first row of apps on the office's home screen. The one that's next pulses, and each one hands you to the next.

- **Marketplace.** Ten sponsorships to sell, from the jersey patch and the field to pouring rights and the radio broadcast. Reach out to a company, call to warm them up, and sit down once their interest reaches 80. Star a company to save it to a sponsorship. A sit-down is a negotiation: nothing tells you what a choice will do, so read what they say and shake before their patience runs out.
- **Game Day.** The ticket, parking, food, the crews, a promotion, and who sits in your box. Every setting pulls two ways: money now, or the fans and the building. You get your staff's guess before the game and what really happened after it.
- **Coach.** The paper's writers ask you questions each week. What you say moves something, and it shows up in the next issue.
- **Send.** Closes the week. It says exactly what goes into your save. Most weeks, nothing does.

Around the week:

- **You have 100 hours a week** in your first year and 60 after it. Unused hours are gone at Send.
- **The board sets a goal** each year from what you could sell. Beat it and next year's budget is bigger. A first year has no goal.
- **Your staff.** Theo, your account manager, is the one who gives advice, and he is sometimes wrong. Your market analyst and your game day director give none: they set how tight a number is. A sponsorship's worth is a range, and a better analyst gives a tighter one. You hire them at the Winter Meetings.
- **Discover.** What fans, your partners, other schools and the paper's writers are saying about your program this week. Every line comes from your save and from what you did.
- **The year.** Bowl weeks, the Recap that grades your year, and the Winter Meetings, where you set up the next one: the conference's votes, the TV deal, your hires, even a job at another school.

## Before you start

- **Back up your dynasty.** Copy your save folder somewhere safe (`Documents\EA SPORTS College Football 27\saves`, and `Madden NFL 27\saves` if you play both). S2S makes its own backup before every write, but a copy of your own costs nothing.
- **The mods S2S is built around.** The [AIO Dynasty Mod](https://discord.com/channels/1479669373244407929/1488252737815580712/threads/1562974043865616394) (the All in One, which includes Zip's Economy Overhaul) and the [Stadium Experience Mod](https://discord.com/channels/1479669373244407929/1488252737815580712/threads/1530381168879599636) (the sponsors on the stadium walls). AD mode runs with or without them. On a league that runs the All in One, your school's Academic and Campus grades are the mod's and S2S leaves them alone.

## Install (about 5 minutes)

1. **Unzip** the S2S folder somewhere you'll keep it, like `C:\S2S`.
2. **Double-click `S2S.bat`.** If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info ▸ Run anyway**. If Node.js is missing it offers to install it. The first start takes about a minute. When Windows asks about **Node.js** and the firewall, tick both boxes and click **Allow**: that's what lets your phone reach it.
3. **Your browser opens on the setup:**
   1. **Your save:** S2S finds it. Pick the AUTOSAVE. Got both games? Add the other one too and they link into one universe.
   2. **The writer:** **Connect ChatGPT** (free). One tap: ChatGPT's sign-in opens in your PC's browser, and the setup moves on once you're in (the first time it also installs the writer, a minute or two).
   3. **Your phone:** scan the code on the same Wi-Fi, then Add to Home Screen.

Leave the S2S window open while you play. Closing it stops the app.

## What to try

1. **A normal week.** Play a week, advance, and tap **Up to date**, the light at the top of your phone (it reads **Write** when a week is waiting). The first one only files the week; from the next advance on, every one writes the issue. Read it on your phone.
2. **Start AD mode.** Tap **Office** in the bottom bar. It opens once S2S has read a college week. Three short cards explain it, then **Start AD mode**. AD mode is college only.
3. **A week as the AD.** Follow the pulsing app: Marketplace, Game Day, Coach, Send. Then play your game and advance. The next week opens when the save moves on.
4. **Sell something.** Your first deal takes a few weeks: reach out, call twice a week, sit down at 80. The two brands marked as the owner's own start well up the bar, if you want a quick one.
5. **Look around.** Discover, and **Learn more** under the bar: Money, the Arc, Your staff, Your storyline, and How it works.
6. **Your phone.** Do a whole week from the couch.

## What AD mode writes into your save, and how to undo it

Only **Send** writes, and it says what before you tap it. There are two things it can write:

- **Program points, once a year.** In the preseason you choose how much of last year's raise goes into the game. That part is written into your school's budget as program points at your next Send.
- **The league's grades, only if you turn them on.** A switch on the Arc's screen, off to start. On, each Send moves your school's grades with your business and every other school's with its results. Turn it off and the next Send takes S2S's moves back out.

Every other Send writes nothing at all.

- S2S refuses while the game is running. Close the game, send, then open it again.
- It backs the save up before every write, and reads every change back. If one doesn't stick it puts the backup back and the week stays unsent.
- If a write can't be made, you can send the week without it. What is owed stays owed until a later Send.
- **To undo a write:** close the game and copy the backup S2S made over your save, or use your own copy.

## Known gaps

- **Discover is written from templates, not by the writer.** It is true to your league, but its phrasing repeats over a season.
- **Meet** on the Coach screen is a card marked beta. Team meetings and 1-on-1s are not in this AD mode.
- **The other schools' athletic directors** don't run budgets of their own yet. They do compete for your sponsors.
- **Your budget** pays your staff and Theo, and the game's part buys program points. Nothing else is bought with it yet.
- **Company logos** are small where S2S has one, and about a third of the companies have none.
- Some numbers in AD mode are S2S's own model, not the game's.

## Found something? Post it in the S2S thread

**Settings ▸ Support ▸ Report a problem ▸ Copy diagnostics**, then paste it in the [S2S thread on Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1479669373244407929/1562241150407151727) with:

- what you did, and what you expected
- a screenshot (on your phone is fine)

The diagnostics carry versions, your leagues' weeks and the last run's log. They never include your save, a key or a sign-in.

Things worth reporting: anything that looks wrong in the paper or in Discover, a deal that makes no sense, a write that didn't show up in the game, a step that confused you, and anything you loved.
