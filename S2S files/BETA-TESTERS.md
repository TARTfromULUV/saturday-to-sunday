# S2S beta: the Front Office (AD mode) and Coach mode

Thanks for testing. S2S writes a weekly sports paper about your CFB 27 dynasty and Madden 27 franchise, and you read it on your phone. This beta adds the **Front Office**: you run the athletic department (**AD mode**) and the sideline (**Coach mode**), and the paper covers what you decide.

**AD Mode: run the business side of your program.** You're the athletic director: the businessman. The coaching (game plans, recruiting, the depth chart) still happens in the game. AD Mode reads what you do there and doesn't run it. Your job is the business: raise money and build the relationships that pay for everything.

- **Sell your sponsorships.** Pouring rights, the jersey patch, the stadium's boards, concessions. A pouring partner shuts out the other drinks: who you sign decides who still fits.
- **Work the deals.** Send your partnerships VP after a company, hold out for a better round, or take the call when a brand comes to you. The better your program, the more brands answer.
- **Keep your partners happy.** Sponsors want things from you: a signing, a radio hour, a post. Show up, or they'll remember at renewal.
- **Answer to the board.** Your grades and your program's prestige set how fast you can grow. A small school can climb, just not overnight.
- *New:* hire your business staff in tiers, open a slot to bids, sell next season's deals from week 8, regional and national brands, your fans' favorites, and an AD score paid as credits at the Recap.

At the end of each season, the Recap grades your year and turns it into money for the next one, written into your save.

## Before you start

- **Back up your dynasty.** Copy your save folder somewhere safe (`Documents\EA SPORTS College Football 27\saves`, and `Madden NFL 27\saves` if you play both). The Front Office makes its own backups, but a copy of your own costs nothing.
- **Get the two mods S2S is built around.** The [AIO Dynasty Mod](https://discord.com/channels/1479669373244407929/1488252737815580712/threads/1562974043865616394) (the All in One, which includes Zip's Economy Overhaul) and the [Stadium Experience Mod](https://discord.com/channels/1479669373244407929/1488252737815580712/threads/1530381168879599636) (the sponsors on the stadium walls, which S2S's Marketplace sells).
- **S2S is built on Zip's All in One mod** (with his Economy Overhaul). The Booster Club, its donors and the mod's economy are what the office's money runs on; without the mod you get a lighter office on S2S's own money model. Use the mod, and read "If you run Zip's mods" below before you Send anything.

## Install (about 5 minutes)

1. **Unzip** the S2S folder somewhere you'll keep it, like `C:\S2S`.
2. **Double-click `S2S.bat`.** If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info ▸ Run anyway**. If Node.js is missing it offers to install it. The first start takes about a minute. When Windows asks about **Node.js** and the firewall, tick both boxes and click **Allow**: that's what lets your phone reach it.
3. **Your browser opens on the setup:**
   1. **Your save:** S2S finds it. Pick the AUTOSAVE. Got both games? Add the other one too and they link into one universe.
   2. **The writer:** **Connect ChatGPT** (free). One tap: ChatGPT's sign-in opens in your PC's browser, and the setup moves on once you're in (the first time it also installs the writer, a minute or two).
   3. **Your phone:** scan the code on the same Wi-Fi, then Add to Home Screen.

Leave the S2S window open while you play. Closing it stops the app.

## What to try

1. **A normal week.** Play a week, advance, and hit **Generate** (the first one only files the week; from the next advance on, every Generate writes the issue). Read it on your phone.
2. **A week as the AD.** Tap **Office** in the bottom bar (tagged BETA). It opens once S2S has read a college week. Answer the week's calls, **Send** what goes into the game, play the week, and see what the paper says.
3. **A week as the coach.** Turn Coach mode on from the office's **Send** page (it starts the next week). The Front Office is college-only for now. Make the calls: what can go into the save is Sent with the rest of the week; the rest goes on your **To do in the game** list, and the next read checks it off.
4. **Your phone.** Do a whole week from the couch: the paper, the office, the calls.

## What the Front Office writes, and how to undo it

Only what you **Send**: AD mode's pay, budget and staff moves, and Coach mode's team meetings, 1-on-1s (a small change to players' hidden confidence) and talent tree. Recruiting is read only in this beta: S2S reads your board and never writes it.

- S2S refuses while the game is running. Close the game, send, then open it again.
- Before every send it backs the save up: the last five, in an `S2S` folder next to your save file.
- It reads every change back, and if one doesn't stick it puts the backup back.
- **To undo a Send:** close the game, then **Your week ▸ Details ▸ Put back the save from before week N's Send** (or copy that backup from the `S2S` folder over your save yourself). S2S sees the save is the one from before the Send, reopens the week and deals it again, so you can answer and Send again. If you put back some other older copy and the week still says sent, tap **Reopen this week** in the same place.

Free agency, trades, cuts and captains are never written: they're on your To do list for the game.

### If you run Zip's mods (All in One, Economy Overhaul)

That's the way S2S is meant to be played, and **Send works on a modded league**. Every Send backs the save up first and reads each change back, so a week can always be put back (Your week ▸ Details lists the backups).

One kind of change is held on a modded league: recruiting offers and scholarships. The mod's boosters move the same recruits, so make those in the game. The Send page says "held for now" beside anything it leaves out.

## Known gaps

- The playoff calls have only been tested on a made-up playoff week.
- Free agency, trades, cuts and captains end in "do it in the game" (trades have no check yet).
- A college league's offseason is the AD's. The coach's college calls are in-season only.
- Some numbers in AD mode are S2S's own model, not the game's.

## Found something? Post it in the S2S thread

**Settings ▸ Support ▸ Report a problem ▸ Copy diagnostics**, then paste it in the [S2S thread on Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1479669373244407929/1562241150407151727) with:

- what you did, and what you expected
- a screenshot (on your phone is fine)

The diagnostics carry versions, your leagues' weeks and the last run's log. They never include your save, a key or a sign-in.

Things worth reporting: anything that looks wrong in the paper, a call that makes no sense, a write that didn't show up in the game, a step in the setup that confused you, and anything you loved.
