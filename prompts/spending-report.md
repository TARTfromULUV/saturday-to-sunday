# The spending report

Where college football's money went this year, from the programs' own books:
every school spends its budget across NIL (its roster's deals), recruiting,
its coaching staff and its facilities. Who went all-in on players, who is
buying recruits, who is paying coaches, who is building, and whether any of
it is showing up anywhere. A business feature, not a table.

500 to 800 words.

## What you are given

- `when`: `midseason`, or `after signing day` (the new class's deals are now
  on the books).
- `howDollarsWork`: how this paper prices the game's spending in dollars.
  Use the dollar figures as given; never explain the conversion beyond one
  plain clause if you must ("by this paper's count").
- `nilLeaders`: the five biggest NIL rosters, with `nilRosterDollars`.
- `recruitingLeaders`, `staffLeaders`: the five biggest spenders on
  recruiting and on the coaching staff, in dollars.
- `biggestDeals`: the five biggest individual NIL deals: player, position,
  school, dollars.
- `building`: programs putting money into facilities: dollars, whether an
  upgrade is under way (`upgrading` names it) or landed last year, and the
  facilities level (0-4 in the game's terms; say "top-tier" for 4).
- `gradeMoves`: My School grades that moved (facilities, brand exposure,
  stadium atmosphere, program tradition, championship contender), with the
  letter before and after.
- `yourProgram`, when given: the reader's school's own spending.

## Structure

1. **The open**: the one number that defines the year (the biggest roster,
   the biggest deal, or the biggest bet), in two or three sentences.
2. **The NIL arms race**: the top rosters and the biggest deals by name.
3. **Different bets**: who is spending on recruiting, who on staff, and
   what that says about where each program thinks its next win comes from.
4. **Building**: facilities spending and upgrades; when a facilities or
   atmosphere grade moved in `gradeMoves`, connect the two plainly ("and its
   athletic facilities grade went from B+ to A").
5. **Your program**, when `yourProgram` is given: where it sits, in one
   paragraph.
6. Close forward: what this spending sets up for next season.

## Rules

- Every dollar figure, name and grade comes from what you are given, exactly.
  Round as given; never total, average or compare numbers yourself beyond
  what the fields state.
- Money does not buy wins. Never claim it did; a grade that moved after
  spending is the most you may connect.
- No donors, collectives, agents or sources. These are the programs' own
  books, nothing more.
- Never mention points, a game, a save, a mod or a tool.
