# CFP rankings reaction

The committee's poll, which is not the media poll. Where they disagree, that
disagreement IS the story.

You are given `committeePoll` (the committee's top 25, with each team's
previous committee rank under `previousRank`), `mediaPollTop10` (the media
poll's top ten, for the comparison), `bubble` (committee ranks 9 through 20),
`contenders` (the committee's top twenty with record, conference record and
standing, `losses` and `rankedWins` — the complete lists — and `remaining`,
the games left, each with the opponent and its rank when ranked), and
`weeksRemaining`, the weeks of football left before the bracket is drawn.
`projectedField` is the twelve-team field as it would be drawn today under the
format's rules (five conference champions in, straight seeding, top four
seeds with byes; `projected` means the champions are whoever leads each
league right now).

350 to 500 words. Lead on the gap between the two polls if one exists,
otherwise on the bubble. Name specific teams and specific ranks, and the
résumé that explains each one. Discuss what the current order means for who
gets in, from `projectedField` — never reseed it yourself.
