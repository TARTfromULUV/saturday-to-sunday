# A player with a following

One of the program's players has an audience of his own: more than 100,000
followers. This is a feature for the money desk about who he is online, why
people follow him, and what the brands pay him: a campus beat writer's
profile, light on its feet.

## What you are given

- `team`: the school.
- `player`: `name`, `position`, `year` (class), `hometown`, `followers`
  (all platforms), `gainedThisSeason`, `tiktok`, `instagram`, `x`, and
  `persona`, the short reason he stands out:
  - `Viral`: an international kicker or punter, a story on its own.
  - `QB1`: the starting quarterback.
  - `Highlights`: the big plays.
  - `Hometown`: a kid from the program's home state.
  - `Star`: a four- or five-star talent.
- `player.why`: the facts behind it ("a punter from Sydney, leg power 83",
  "29 touchdowns this season", "a 74-yard play"). Use these and nothing else
  for what he has done.
- `player.brandDeals`: his brand deals this season (`brand`, `pay` in
  millions, `years`). The money is his, not the department's.
- `teamBrandMoney` (millions, all the program's player deals this season),
  `teamDeals` (how many), `rank` (his place among the program's followings).

## The shape

300 to 500 words. A headline that names him and the number ("The punter with
148,000 followers").

**1. The hook.** Who he is and the number. If he is `Viral`, start where he
is from.

**2. Why people follow him**, from `why`. Describe the kind of content plainly
(highlight clips, practice videos, a punt-challenge series), without inventing
a specific post, view count or date.

**3. The brands.** Each deal: the brand, what it is for, the money. If there
are none yet, say brands have not come calling yet.

**4. What it means for the program.** One paragraph: a following like his is
something recruits notice and the department can point to. Measured: never say
it changed a recruit's decision, attendance or a ranking.

## Rules

- Numbers exactly as given. Followers rounded the way a newspaper would
  ("148,500" or "nearly 150,000"). Money in dollars ("$45,000").
- No quotes. Nobody was interviewed.
- Never invent his handle, a post, a teammate's reaction or a coach's view.
- A brand is a partner like any other: report the deal, never sell the
  product. CPG Playbook included.
- Never say the game is a game, a save or a file. It is the season.
