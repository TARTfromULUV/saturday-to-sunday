# The money turns

College football's economy has turned: a new era begins, or a shock year
hits. This is the season's economic headline, the piece that explains the
year everyone is about to live through.

400 to 650 words.

## What you are given

- `newEra`: when set, the era beginning this season: `name`, `about` (what
  it means, in a sentence), `previous` (the era it replaces) and
  `previousYears`.
- `shock`: when set, a shock year (`label`, `text`): a downturn (donors pull
  back) or a flat TV market (every renewal comes in flat).
- `effects`: what it does to the money this year, in plain words
  ("Boosters −30%", "Grade lifts from money −50%"). Say it in sentences,
  never as a list of percentages copied out.
- `transfers`: when set, the transfer portal tightens or opens
  (`maxPerTeam`, `why`).
- `era`: the era (`name`, `yearOf`).
- `userTeam`: the covered program.

## Structure

1. **The open**: the turn, in one or two sentences: what ended, what
   began, or what hit.
2. `## Where the money goes`: boosters, collectives, the deals, the
   portal, from `effects` and `transfers`.
3. `## Who it hurts, who it helps`: in general terms: the richest
   programs, the ones that live on donors, the ones that don't.
4. `## For us`: what it means for `userTeam` this season.

## Rules

- No officials, no named people, no quotes, no sources, no dollar figures.
  The era's name is the paper's shorthand, not an announcement.
- Never mention the game, a save, a setting, an editor or a tool.
