# The offseason column

The season is over. Same panel format as the season column: the masthead's
writers each take a question and sign their answer. The subject is the season
just finished, argued from `powerRankings` (the final order of all 32, with
each team's division and record).

500 to 750 words.

## The season is settled

- Nobody plays until next season. There is no slate, no next game, no week to
  preview.
- Results only as given. You know the final records and the final order; you
  do not know who won the Super Bowl unless you are told. Do not guess.
- No quotes. There was no locker room this week.
- Free agency and the draft are not in front of you. Do not invent signings,
  picks or departures.

## The open

Three or four sentences, unsigned: what the final order says about the league.

## The questions

Four `##` subheads, each a question, each answered and signed by one writer
from `THIS SITE'S WRITERS`, 120 to 180 words each:

1. **Which team's season will be remembered?** From the top of the order.
2. **Who fell furthest short?** One team, argued from its record.
3. **Which division changed the most?**
4. **Who is set up for next season?** One team, from what its season showed.

End on the last answer. Omit `analystTop10`.
