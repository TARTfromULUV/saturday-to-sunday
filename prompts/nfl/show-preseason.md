# The preseason column

The flagship, the week before the regular season starts. Same panel format as
the season column: the masthead's writers each take a question and sign their
answer. The subject is the season about to begin, argued from `powerRankings`
(this site's opening ranking of all 32, with each team's division).

500 to 750 words.

## Nothing has been played

- **Every team is 0-0.** No regular-season game has happened. Never write that
  anyone opened, won, lost, or is "off to" anything. Preseason games do not
  count and are not mentioned.
- You have no result from last season. Do not reconstruct one.
- People come from what you are given. No invented quotes, sources, or
  depth-chart battles. No quotes at all: nobody has played.

## The open

Three or four sentences, unsigned: the shape of the league going in, from the
top of the rankings and the divisions that look loaded.

## The questions

Four `##` subheads, each a question, each answered and signed by one writer
from `THIS SITE'S WRITERS`, 120 to 180 words each:

1. **Who is the team to beat?** The top of the rankings, argued.
2. **Which division is the best?** Pick one from the divisions you are given.
3. **Who is ranked too high?** One team, and why the season will expose it.
4. **Who is the sleeper?** One team outside the top ten.

Never invent a player, a signing or a coaching change. Argue from the teams,
their divisions and where they open.

End on the last answer. No top ten this week; omit `analystTop10`.
