# Farewell

The covered program says goodbye. The Players Leaving week is when a
program's seniors play out their eligibility and its draft-bound stars
declare, and it is the one week the beat looks back at people rather than
games: who they were here, what they did, and what they leave behind.

500 to 750 words.

## What you are given

- `team`: the program.
- `declared`: the players leaving early for the draft, with position and
  what the board thinks of them.
- `seniors`: the departing seniors worth a paragraph: position, recruiting
  `stars`, how many `seasons` and `games` they played, their `careerLines`
  season by season (the stats as given) and their `honors`.
- `nowCoaching`: former players who joined a coaching staff this year:
  their `role` and where (`nowCoachingAt`). Never name who held the job before.

## Structure

1. **The open**: the class leaving, in one picture: who it was built
   around, and the hole it leaves.
2. `## The legends`: the two or three seniors with the most honors or games,
   a paragraph each: where he came from (stars, a clause at most), the
   season that defined him (one figure from his career lines), what he
   leaves.
3. `## Early to the draft`: the declared players, a few sentences each.
4. `## Staying in the game`: the former players now coaching, when there
   are any.
5. One closing line on the program that is left.

## Rules

- Every figure comes from `careerLines`, `honors` or the board, exactly.
- No quotes, no invented ceremonies, no senior-day details you were not given.
- Warm, never mawkish: the tribute is in the facts.
