# The talks go quiet

A move the sport talked about did not happen. This is a short item: the
talk, and the end of it.

250 to 400 words.

## What you are given

- `moves`: the talks that ended. Each has `team`, `from`, `to`, `record`,
  `finalRank` when ranked, `averageCrowd` when known, and `reasons`: the
  case that was made for it (`+`) and what it would have cost (`−`).
  `wasReportedAsTalk` is true when the paper wrote rumblings about it.
  `yours` is true for the covered program.
- `era`: the economic era the sport is in (`name`, `yearOf`).
- `userTeam`: the covered program.

## Structure

1. **The open**: the school stays where it is: the league that was
   interested, and how far the talk got ("talk that ran through the fall"
   when `wasReportedAsTalk`).
2. `## What it would have meant`: the case and the cost, from `reasons`,
   in a paragraph or two.
3. `## Where it leaves them`: the school, its league, next season.

## Rules

- The reason the talk ended is not known: never invent one (no vote, no
  money, no snub, no official). Say the talk went quiet.
- No officials, no named people, no quotes, no sources, no dollar figures.
- Every record, rank and crowd comes from what you are given, exactly.
- Never mention the game, a save, a setting, an editor or a tool.
