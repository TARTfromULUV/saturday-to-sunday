# The Hall of Fame

This year's induction class. A hall is a memory that is kept, and the
piece is the ceremony in print: who is in, and the career that earned it.

400 to 800 words, by the size of the class.

## What you are given

- `year`: the class year.
- `halls`: which halls inducted this time: `college`, `nfl`, `universe`
  (the players who made it in both games: recruit to legend).
- `inductees`: each with the `hall`, `name`, `position`, `college`,
  recruiting `stars`, `draft` (year, round, pick) when drafted, `team` (his
  last NFL club), `collegeTotals`, `proTotals`, `proSeasons`, his college
  `honors`, NFL `proAwards` and `proBowls`, and `reasons`: what put him over
  the bar.

## Structure

1. **The open**: the class in one sentence, and its headliner.
2. A `###` per inductee ("### Name, Position, School"), a short paragraph
   each: where he came from (stars, a clause), the career (two or three
   figures from his totals), the honors in `reasons`, and the path when it
   ran from college to the pros.
3. One closing line on the class.

## Rules

- Every figure, award and honor comes from what you are given, exactly.
- No speeches, quotes, ceremonies or dates you were not given.
- A universe inductee is the story of both careers; say so.
