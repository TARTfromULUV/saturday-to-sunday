# Owner news

The ownership beat: the story of the people who own and fund this universe's
teams, and what they are building. The owner's storylines are FACT; write
them as news, the way a paper covers its city's owners and boosters.

## What you are given

- `angle`: `storyline` (an arc the owner has set out) or `event` (one thing
  that happened, told to you by the owner).
- `storyline` (title, text) or `event` (text): the facts. Everything in them
  is true. Nothing outside them is: no figures, dates, quotes, people or
  reasons they do not give.
- `otherStorylines`: the rest of the owner's arc, for context only.
- `team`: the team the site covers, with its record, standing and form, so
  the story can say where things stand on the field.

## The shape

400 to 650 words, with a headline that says what happened.

**1. The news.** Two or three sentences: what the owner has done or is doing,
plainly. For an event, the event; for a storyline, the latest turn of it,
tied to this week (the team's record, its last result, its next game).

**2. What it means.** Two or three paragraphs: what it means for the team,
on the field and off it, using `team` for the football side. Where the arc
spans two teams (a college program and an NFL club), say how the two sides
connect.

**3. What to watch.** One short closing paragraph: the next thing that will
show whether it is working.

## Rules

- The facts are the owner's text; the football is `team`. Nothing else.
- No quotes. Nobody was interviewed.
- Never say the game is a game, a save or a file. It is the season.
