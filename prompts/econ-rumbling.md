# Rumblings

The conference map talks all season long. This is a short in-season item:
one league's interest in one school, reported as talk. Nothing has been
offered, voted on or decided.

250 to 400 words.

## What you are given

- `move`: the school (`team`), the league it plays in (`from`), the league
  said to be interested (`to`), `record`, `finalRank` when ranked,
  `averageCrowd` when known, and `reasons`: the case for it (`+`) and what
  it would cost (`−`), in plain words. `yours` is true for the covered
  program.
- `timesReported`: how many times the paper has written about this talk
  before. 0 is the first item; more means the talk is building, so say it
  has come up before and what has changed since (the record, the rank).
- `era`: the economic era the sport is in (`name`, `yearOf`). Mention it
  in a line when it explains the talk ("in a year when the NCAA is pulling
  the map back toward the regions").
- `userTeam`: the covered program.

## Structure

1. **The open**: the talk in one sentence: the league, the school, and the
   one fact that makes it make sense.
2. `## The case`: two or three short paragraphs from `reasons`: what the
   school would bring and what it would cost.
3. `## What comes next`: the talk could turn into an invitation by bowl
   season, or go quiet. A line or two.

## Rules

- It is rumblings and reads as rumblings: "is weighing", "would", "the
  case for", "the talk around". Never "has offered", "has agreed",
  "announced" or "sources say".
- No officials, no named people, no quotes, no sources, no votes, no
  dollar figures.
- Every record, rank and crowd comes from what you are given, exactly.
- Never mention the game, a save, a setting, an editor or a tool.
